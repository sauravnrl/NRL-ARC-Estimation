View methodology, safeguards and database details
Historical LPP adjustment: baseline basic LPP × (1 + applicable EED annualized rate)actual elapsed years. The 3-year route is preferred; the 5-year route requires appropriate approval and an available 5-year comparable category. Future fixed-rate ARC: Adjusted LPP is adopted directly as the Year 1 price. Year 2 = Adjusted LPP × (1 + future annual rate); Year 3 = Adjusted LPP × (1 + future annual rate)². The future rate is selected using item-wise WPI rates provided by NRL EED, a uniform All Commodities rate where no specific commodity reference is available, or zero future escalation. These are planning assumptions, not notified future rates. Contract-year prices are averaged equally because quantities are not provided. Matching: all mechanical-material descriptions are checked against the embedded commodity keyword mapping. For every item, the historical rate period is auto-selected from LPP age (up to 3 years: 3-Year; above 3 and up to 5 years: 5-Year) and remains editable by the user. The selected period is validated against LPP age, category-rate availability and approval. All EED categories remain available for manual category selection.
